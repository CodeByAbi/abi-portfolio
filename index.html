About

01   About

Abira Wisnunggal Call me Abi.

I'm a final-year Data Science undergraduate at Nusa Mandiri University (GPA 3.73 / 4.00). For the past 1.5 years I've been building LLM applications, mostly RAG and Text-to-SQL, alongside classical ML models for classification and forecasting.

Most of that work is backend: retrieval pipelines (hybrid RAG, knowledge graphs, pgvector), Text-to-SQL with validation rules, and async services on FastAPI, Celery, RabbitMQ and Docker. I've also produced ground-truth annotation for autonomous driving and built BI dashboards in Looker Studio and Tableau.

I'm currently an AI Engineer Intern at BRIN, building question answering over a bibliometrics database. I'm looking for an AI engineering internship or junior role.
